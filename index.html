Where Vision Meets Action

Saudi Arabia is not waiting for the future — it's building it. LEAD is the world's largest AI education and creation initiative, designed to train and empower over 15 million Saudis with hands-on experience in today's most advanced AI tools. From engineering and design to storytelling and strategy, LEAD turns learning into action and ambition into achievement.

Aligned with Vision 2030, LEAD brings together government, the private sector, and the world's leading AI companies to make innovation part of daily life. It's not about studying technology — it's about using it to build smarter businesses, better cities, and a more creative nation ready to lead globally.

The Perfect Ground for a Global Transformation

Saudi Arabia's commitment to technology is comprehensive — digital infrastructure, policy, and investment are aligned to build an AI-driven economy. With ~99% internet penetration and a population where ~69–71% are under 35, the Kingdom combines near-universal connectivity with a dynamic, future-oriented population.

The government's alignment around Vision 2030 ensures innovation is supported and scaled at every level, while global partnerships and long-term investments in education position the Kingdom to become a primary center for AI development and application.

A National Platform for Learning, Building, and Launching

LEAD convenes the world's top AI companies — OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Amazon, Midjourney, Runway, Adobe, and more — to teach and train across the full spectrum of AI: from technical depth (coding, automation, data systems) to practical creativity (design, media, business workflows).

Learn

Focused, hands-on sessions using real tools to solve Saudi-relevant problems in engineering, design, storytelling, and business operations.

Build

The national LEAD Hackathon: a high-intensity sprint where teams apply their new skills to concrete briefs and deliver working systems and creative outputs.

Launch

Top projects move into government and corporate pilots, a 90-day venture studio, and hiring pathways with leading employers.

The Nationwide Creation Sprint

The LEAD Hackathon is where learning becomes invention — a Kingdom-wide sprint uniting developers, artists, entrepreneurs, and professionals to build Saudi Arabia's next generation of solutions.
