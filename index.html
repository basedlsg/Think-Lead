The LEAD Program

A National Platform for Learning, Building, and Launching

LEAD convenes the world's top AI companies — OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Amazon, Midjourney, Runway, Adobe, and more — to teach and train across the full spectrum of AI: from technical depth to practical creativity.

01

Learn

Focused, hands-on sessions using real tools to solve Saudi-relevant problems in engineering, design, storytelling, and business operations.

02

Build

The national LEAD Hackathon: a high-intensity sprint where teams apply their new skills to concrete briefs and deliver working systems.

03

Launch

Top projects move into government and corporate pilots, a 90-day venture studio, and hiring pathways with leading employers.

The Hackathon

The Nationwide Creation Sprint

The LEAD Hackathon is where learning becomes invention — a Kingdom-wide sprint uniting developers, artists, entrepreneurs, and professionals to build Saudi Arabia's next generation of solutions.

LEFT BRAIN

BUILD

Logic, engineering, and systems

What gets built:

  • AI agents
  • Business automation
  • Domain apps (energy, healthcare, education)
  • Data products and integrations

What judges look for:

Reliability, utility, integration readiness, measurable benefit to users or operations.

RIGHT BRAIN

CREATE

Imagination, art, and storytelling

What gets created:

  • Generative video and imagery
  • Audio experiences
  • Brand systems
  • Cultural and heritage storytelling
  • Next-gen media formats

What judges look for:

Originality, craft, audience impact, cultural relevance, responsible use.
